✖️ Multiplication Table Quiz

Setup

No x1 questions and no repeats (3x7 and 7x3 are the same). Questions are spread across the tables, and at most one question uses 10. Easy: at least one factor 2-5 · Medium: both factors 4 or more · Hard: both factors 6 or more, with close answer options. If a level has too few questions, the next-nearest level fills the gap. Reverse: a number is shown and the student gives the factor pairs (factors 2 or more, up to the larger of the highest table and rows). Every finished quiz is saved automatically.
